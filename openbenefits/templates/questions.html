Before you start

You do not need any documents to use this tool. Having these details in mind will help you answer quickly:

  • Your age or age group (for example, “around 20”, “above 60”).
  • Whether you are studying, working, looking for work or retired.
  • A rough idea of your family’s annual income (for example, “around ₹3 lakh”).
  • Whether your home is in a village or a town/city.

This tool gives an initial indication only. Final eligibility is always decided by the scheme owner when you apply.
