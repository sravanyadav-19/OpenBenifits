Find government schemes that may help you.

OpenBenefits is a simple tool. Answer a few basic questions and see which government schemes might match your situation.

  • Made for students, parents and working people
  • Only basic details are asked (age, work, income, area)
  • Shows a clear list of schemes you can read more about
Start eligibility check

Note: This is a demo tool. It gives guidance only. Please always confirm details on the official government website before applying.

How it works

The process is quick and simple. It usually takes less than one minute.

1. Answer a few questions

We ask about your age, if you are a student or working, your approximate family income and whether you live in an urban or rural area.

2. We match schemes

OpenBenefits compares your answers with a list of government schemes and checks where you may fit the basic rules.

3. You see the results

You get a short list of schemes with a simple explanation and links to read official information.

Why this tool is useful

Many people do not know about schemes they could use. This tool helps you discover options in a simple way.

Easy to read

Information is written in plain language, not legal or technical terms, so parents and students can read it easily.

Clear reasons

For each scheme, the page explains why it appears in your results based on the answers you gave.

Works on any device

You can use OpenBenefits on a phone, tablet or computer, at home or at a counselling centre.

About OpenBenefits

OpenBenefits is a demonstration made for the Google TechSprint on Open Innovation in Government. It shows how scheme information can be made easier to use through simple rules and clear explanations.

This is not an official government website. The list of schemes is limited and simplified. Before you apply for any scheme, always read the latest information on the official government portal.
