A SHORT, CLEAR PROCESS

How it works

The process is simple and usually takes less than a minute.

01

1. Answer a few questions

We ask about your age, whether you are studying or working, your approximate family income and whether you live in an urban or rural area.

02

2. We match schemes

OpenBenefits compares your answers with a rules file of government schemes and checks where you may fit the basic conditions.

03

3. You see the results

You get a short list of schemes with a clear explanation of why each scheme appears and links to read official information.

DESIGNED FOR CLARITY

Why this tool is useful

Many people do not know which schemes may fit them. This tool helps you discover options in a simple, transparent way.

Easy to read

Information is written in plain language, not legal or technical terms, so parents and students can understand it easily.

Clear reasons

For each scheme, the page explains why it appears in your results based on the answers you gave.

Works on any device

You can use OpenBenefits on a phone, tablet or computer, at home or at a counselling centre.

ABOUT THE DEMO

About OpenBenefits

OpenBenefits is a demonstration made for the Google TechSprint on Open Innovation in Government. It shows how scheme information can be turned into simple rules and clear explanations.

This is not an official government website. The list of schemes is limited and simplified. Before you apply for any scheme, always read the latest information on the official government portal.
